**Overview**

This case study presents a Proof of Concept (PoC) developed to evaluate how AI and IoT-enabled vehicle telematics can improve customer outcomes, enhance operational visibility, and enable more informed decision-making across the automotive ecosystem.
The initiative focuses on transforming how vehicle data is captured, analyzed, and utilized to address real-world challenges faced by drivers, manufacturers, and insurers.

**Background & Context**

A U.S.-based automobile manufacturer is seeking to modernize its technology landscape by leveraging advanced analytics to strengthen customer satisfaction and brand reputation. 
The broader objective is to create a more connected, data-driven ecosystem that supports both customer experience and long-term business growth.

**Opportunity Analysis**

The existing ecosystem presents several structural challenges. Customers frequently encounter high and unpredictable repair costs due to delayed maintenance and unexpected vehicle breakdowns, with typical repair expenses ranging between $500 and $1000 per incident. These disruptions impact both cost and convenience.
At the same time, manufacturers have limited visibility into vehicle performance once vehicles are in use, restricting their ability to proactively address issues or improve product quality based on real-world data.
Insurers also face challenges in accurately assessing risk. Without access to reliable driver behavior data, premium calculations are often misaligned, contributing to higher accident rates, fraudulent claims, and inefficient allocation of capital.
Collectively, these issues create inefficiencies across the ecosystem, affecting safety, cost, and overall customer experience.

**Proposed Solution**

The PoC proposes an integrated AI + IoT-enabled telematics platform designed to leverage vehicle data to generate actionable insights across the ecosystem.
The solution is centered on capturing real-time data from vehicle systems and enabling:
 - Predictive maintenance capabilities to reduce unexpected breakdowns Manufacturer visibility into post-sale vehicle performance Driver behavior analysis to improve safety outcomes
 - Behavior-based inputs for more accurate insurance assessment
 - The intent is to transform raw vehicle data into decision-oriented insights, enabling proactive actions across stakeholders.

**Expected Benefits**

 - The PoC is expected to demonstrate multiple areas of value.

 - By enabling predictive maintenance, the solution can reduce unexpected breakdowns and associated repair costs. Driver behavior insights can contribute to improved safety outcomes.

 - For insurers, access to behavior-based data enables better alignment of premiums with actual risk. Manufacturers benefit from improved visibility into vehicle performance.

 - The initiative also establishes a foundation for future connected vehicle capabilities.

**Key Risks & Considerations**

 - Data privacy and security concerns

 - Dependency on connectivity and infrastructure

 - Regulatory considerations for telematics data usage

 - Compatibility limitations with older vehicles
